# LinkedIn Post Formatter — Yaya AI

This skill takes the day's content marketing topic and transforms it into a compelling LinkedIn post (or carousel outline) engineered to drive impressions, comments, and profile visits for Yaya AI.

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill after the daily content topic has been determined. It produces a ready-to-post LinkedIn update that sounds authentically human — not like a press release.

## LinkedIn Content Formatting Rules

### Post Types to Choose From
Based on the day's topic, select the best format:

| Topic Type | Best Format |
|---|---|
| Personal story / empathy | Long-form narrative post |
| Data / results / case study | Short post + stat callout |
| Tutorial / how-to | Carousel (numbered slides) |
| Thought leadership | Essay-style post |
| Product feature | Post + demo screenshot |

### Structure: The LinkedIn Hook Formula
LinkedIn shows only the first 2–3 lines before "see more." The hook must be irresistible.

**Hook Patterns that work:**
- The Bold Claim: *"The resume is dead. Here's what's replacing it."*
- The Counterintuitive Stat: *"The average job seeker spends 18 minutes tailoring one resume. Yaya AI does it in 60 seconds."*
- The Relatable Story: *"I watched a friend spend 3 weeks applying to 40 jobs. She heard back from 2. Here's why."*
- The Question: *"What if your resume automatically matched every job description you applied to?"*

### Post Body Rules
- **Line spacing is critical**: single sentences per line, with blank lines between paragraphs for mobile readability
- **3-5 minute read max** for long-form posts
- Use `→` or `•` for lists, NOT hyphens
- End each section with a one-line punch that transitions to the next
- No jargon. Write like a smart, empathetic human, not a brand account

### Carousel Outline Format (when applicable)
If producing a carousel:
- **Slide 1:** Hook (same as post hook)
- **Slides 2–6:** One key idea per slide, max 20 words each
- **Slide 7:** The "So what?" — the big takeaway
- **Slide 8:** CTA slide

### CTA Rules
- ONE clear call to action only
- Options: "Comment below", "Follow for daily insights", "Try Yaya AI free at [link]", "Share this with someone job hunting"
- Never more than one CTA — it dilutes action

### Hashtag Strategy
- 3–5 hashtags max, placed at the END of the post
- Mix: 1 broad (#JobSearch), 1 niche (#AIForJobSeekers), 1 branded (#YayaAI)
- Never mid-post hashtags

### Yaya AI Brand Voice on LinkedIn
- **Tone:** Thoughtful professional who genuinely cares about job seekers
- **Avoid:** Corporate speak, "excited to announce", "thrilled to share"
- **Embrace:** Vulnerability, specific numbers, second-person ("you"), direct advice
