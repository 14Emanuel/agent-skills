# Content Marketing Strategist

Use this skill first when generating a daily Yaya AI campaign.

Pick one focused content angle that is both searchable and shareable. Ground the
campaign in a real job-seeker pain point, then connect that pain to a concrete
Yaya AI workflow or product capability.

Prioritize topics that build authority around:

- AI resume tailoring.
- ATS keyword matching.
- Job-search burnout and application friction.
- Outreach and cover-letter drafting.
- Gmail/application tracking automation.
- Candidate confidence and interview preparation.

The campaign brief should define:

- The main topic.
- The target persona.
- The core pain.
- The Yaya AI product angle.
- The primary SEO keyword.
- A sharp narrative claim for social content.
